# RE-001 v0.6.1 — Reference Experience Review and Design Freeze

**Status:** Design direction recommended for freeze; functional acceptance NOT granted.
**Date:** 2026-10-22
**Source:** User-reviewed screenshots of v0.6.1 Inspect and Design change, prior v0.6 screenshots, and the locked UO-001 contract.
**Next:** RE-001 v0.6.2 Functional Depth & Evidence Integrity.

## Mission and scope

Execution Strategy Optimizer helps an AI platform/operations engineer find an expensive or unreliable workflow decision, design a bounded alternative, evaluate the same labeled cohort, inspect regressions and guardrails, and control a simulated activation/restoration. The north-star measure is fully loaded cost per verified successful outcome, with explicit accounting boundaries. UO-001 is authoritative.

Reference scenario remains a six-stage synthetic IT service desk workflow: receive request, classify, retrieve policy, draft resolution, validate, resolve/escalate. No production credentials or enterprise integration are required.

## Design evolution and lessons

- v0.4: four destinations (Operate, Investigate, Experiments, Releases); strong conceptual coverage but disconnected pages and small static diagram.
- v0.5: change-centered Attention / Workbench / History, with Inspect / Design change / Compare / Release; more coherent journey, but graph and evidence remained shallow.
- v0.6: persistent three-column graph-centered workbench using @xyflow/react and ELK layout, case explorer, contextual inspector, bottom evidence dock. A runtime error exposed an incorrect default ReactFlow import; named import fixed in follow-up package. Lesson: syntax tests alone are not sufficient.
- v0.6.1: larger graph, stronger branching/route highlighting, projected route counts, richer inspector, expanded evidence dock and table, candidate results withheld until evaluation. User reviewed screenshots and approved visual direction.

## Approved direction to preserve

1. Workbench is primary; Attention and History are contextual surfaces.
2. A single persistent, read-only interactive workflow graph spans Inspect, Design change, Compare and Release.
3. Graph is a projection of authoritative policy and execution records, not a drag-to-edit policy editor.
4. Left panel: searchable/selectable cases; center: topology/decisions; right: mode-specific inspector/editor; bottom: evidence dock.
5. Selecting a case/decision must synchronize graph, inspector and evidence.
6. Evidence provenance is visible; projected, replayed, estimated and measured results must never be conflated.
7. UI should be visually compelling but functional: generous graph scale, clear routing labels, readable type, purposeful color, minimal redundant cards.
8. Preserve bounded strategy optimization; avoid arbitrary workflow authoring and platform sprawl.

## Screenshot observations

**Observed in user screenshots:** persistent graph in Inspect and Design change; distinct premium/economy branches; node selection; projected counts (100 economy, 0 premium for unrestricted routing); inspector with selected case and policy context; bottom case table; candidate outcomes labeled awaiting evaluation; more coherent styling.

**Remaining visual refinements:** full graph sometimes extends beyond canvas bounds at initial fit; verify fitView after ELK completes and after panel resize. Minimap unnecessary for this small graph. Small text at desktop screenshot scale; ensure readable 13–16px functional copy. Strengthen baseline/candidate comparison distinction and selected-case context. Validate responsive layout, keyboard focus, and color accessibility.

**Not established by screenshots:** correct runtime-observed routing, genuine per-step attempt traces, source-derived costs, general policy evaluation, successful activation/restoration, full build, and browser QA.

## Scope boundaries

Do NOT introduce a universal model gateway, general graph editor, new backend/microservices, autonomous policy authoring, enterprise SSO, live provider claims, real-time orchestration, or multi-tenant administration. Keep synthetic deterministic execution first.

## Status and acceptance

This is a design review and freeze recommendation, not implementation acceptance. Formal RE-001 v0.6.2 acceptance requires executable tests and browser proof. Prior locked UO-001 remains unchanged.
